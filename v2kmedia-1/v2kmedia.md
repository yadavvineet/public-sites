# Website Development Prompt — V2K Media

Create a modern, professional, responsive corporate website for **V2K Media**, based in **Mayur Vihar Phase-II, Delhi, India**.

## 1. Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** V2K Media  
**Location:** Mayur Vihar Phase-II, Delhi, India  
**Email:** contact@v2k.media  
**Phone:** +91-9716334238

V2K Media is a diversified business providing a range of **online and offline products, services, creative solutions and technology-led offerings**.

The company operates across areas including:

- Print and marketing solutions
- Digital products and online services
- Mobile and web applications
- Custom technology and business solutions
- Corporate gifting
- Branding and promotional solutions
- Bespoke products and services developed according to client requirements

However, **do not turn the website into a detailed services catalogue**.

This website is intended to function as the **corporate/parent website for V2K Media**.

Individual products, services, applications, brands and specialized offerings may have their own websites or landing pages in the future.

The V2K Media corporate website should primarily communicate:

- who the company is;
- the broad areas in which it operates;
- its ability to work across physical and digital mediums;
- its capability to create customized solutions;
- its business-oriented approach;
- and how prospective clients or partners can contact the company.

---

# 2. Overall Positioning

Position V2K Media as a company operating at the intersection of:

**Media + Technology + Print + Business Solutions**

The company should feel versatile rather than narrowly specialized.

Avoid positioning V2K Media purely as:

- a digital marketing agency;
- a software development company;
- a printing press;
- an advertising agency;
- or a corporate gifting company.

Instead communicate that V2K Media develops, provides and coordinates **creative, digital, physical and technology-driven solutions for businesses and individuals**.

A suitable positioning theme would be:

**Ideas. Digital. Print. Solutions.**

Alternative tone:

**From digital experiences to physical products, we turn ideas into practical solutions.**

Do not overuse marketing buzzwords.

The company should appear credible, approachable and modern.

---

Build a compact corporate website.

Recommended pages:

3. **Contact**

# 16. Visual Design Direction

The website should feel:

- premium but not luxurious;
- modern;
- clean;
- corporate;
- creative;
- technology-aware;
- versatile;
- trustworthy.

Avoid the generic "digital marketing agency" appearance.

Do not overload the site with:

- gradients everywhere;
- animated blobs;
- fake dashboards;
- random stock photographs;
- excessive glassmorphism;
- huge blocks of marketing copy.

Prefer:

- strong typography;
- generous white space;
- simple geometric elements;
- subtle motion;
- excellent spacing;
- bold headlines;
- restrained accents.

---

# 17. Colour Direction

Use a modern neutral base.

Suggested palette direction:

- Near-black / charcoal for primary text
- White / off-white background
- One strong accent colour
- One optional secondary accent

The website should work equally well for a technology business, print business and corporate services company.

Avoid making the visual identity look exclusively like:

- a printing company;
- a software startup;
- or an advertising agency.

If no existing V2K Media logo is supplied, initially create a clean **text-based wordmark**:

**v2k Media**

The "v2k" portion can visually carry more weight than "Media".

Do not create an elaborate logo unless separately requested.

Manrope  
Plus Jakarta Sans  
DM Sans

# 20. Animation

Use restrained animations.

Examples:

- subtle reveal on scroll;
- gentle hover movement;
- button transitions;
- navigation transitions;
- small animated lines or geometric objects.

Animations should not affect usability or page speed.

Respect:

`prefers-reduced-motion`

---

# 21. Responsive Design

Design mobile-first and ensure excellent presentation on:

- mobile phones;
- tablets;
- laptops;
- large desktop monitors.

Avoid horizontal overflow.

Headings should resize fluidly.

Cards should stack elegantly on smaller screens.

---

# 22. Accessibility

Use:

- semantic HTML;
- correct heading hierarchy;
- keyboard-accessible navigation;
- sufficiently large click targets;
- clear focus states;
- accessible form labels;
- adequate colour contrast;
- meaningful aria labels where appropriate.

Target WCAG AA standards.

The implementation should:

- be clean and maintainable;
- avoid massive single-file components;
- use reusable components;

# 27. Important Content Restrictions

Do NOT:

- create fake testimonials;
- create fake client logos;
- create fake projects;
- create fake awards;
- invent client counts;
- invent years of experience;

- invent certifications;

The corporate site should intentionally remain broad.

Detailed information about individual services, applications, products or brands will be placed on separate websites or landing pages later.

- revolutionary
- game-changing
- world-class
- disruptive
- industry-leading
- 360-degree
- next-generation
